Glue compression rides the whole mix so the parts sit together instead of stacking up. Bus reverb is one shared room on top of each instrument's own send. A brick-wall limiter always sits last — Master Vol stays live even with the bus bypassed.
Export
Captures a .wav of everything you hear — post master bus, including Master Vol — plus a .mid of the exact notes played, so you can carry the take into a DAW. Both download when you stop.

Not the same as ⏺ Rec in the transport, which captures the chords you play into an editable Song section that stays inside the app. Use that one to build a chart; use this one to get files out.
