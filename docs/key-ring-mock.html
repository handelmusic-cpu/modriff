WHAT TO TRY

Borrowed chords

Tap a card in the BORROWED row. Notes outside E minor become full amber nodes on the ring, and the header counts them. Fm is two notes out; the others are one.

Appearance

Colour or Monotone, each in Dark or Light. Monotone uses one orange accent for what is live or unusual, and tells parts apart by position and label.

Theory Pack

Switch to FREE to see the key ring locked: still moving behind a veil, with the pack sheet one tap away. The sheet shows the pack's list with the ring as a new line.

Mixer

Drag a fader. Tap M or S. Meters follow the playing parts.
