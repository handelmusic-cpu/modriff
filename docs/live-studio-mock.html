LIVE, STUDIO AND MIX

Silent mock at 430 x 932, revision 2. Tap the part strip on Studio, the three dots on a Live widget, the Mix switches, a channel name, the locked EQ.

Decided: Studio strip with eight entries (Melody and Mel Seq each get a page). Live chords: two rows of four split pads, the 7th on top and the triad below, and an eighth pad for borrowed chords. One three-dot button per Live widget. Mix: rows, the Level / Pan / Rev / Dly / Drv / Mix switch, a channel name opens its strip. EQ: five bands (HPF, Lo shelf, Mid, Hi shelf, LPF), shelf corner frequencies, shown locked in a free build. Bass and Mel Seq show play keys only while the roll is empty. Arp stays a settings page. Not drawn on purpose: a per-page Mute, drum play pads, the invented Chain card.
